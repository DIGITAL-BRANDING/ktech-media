Jaridar Waraka TV Ta Karrama Dr. Zahraddin Abdul’aziz da Lambar Yabo ta YOUTH AMBASSADOR

By Comrd. Abdul'aziz Aliyu 14, February 2026 12.4K views

Jaridar Waraka TV ta karrama Dr. Zahraddin Abdul’aziz, Shugaban Rukunin Babban Asibitin Masha Allah Clinic and Maternity Hospital dake Ja’en Masallaci, bisa gudunmawar da yake bayarwa a fannin kiwon lafiya da hidimtawa al’umma. An mika masa lambar yabo ta “jakadan Matasan Najeriya ” a wani taro da aka gudanar cikin yanayi na farin ciki, inda aka yaba da kokarinsa wajen inganta harkokin lafiya da kula da marasa lafiya, musamman mata da kananan yara. A jawabinsa, Dr. Zahraddin ya gode wa Jaridar Waraka TV da wannan karramawa, yana mai jaddada cewa hakan zai kara masa kwarin gwiwa wajen ci gaba da yi wa al’umma hidima. Wasu daga cikin mahalarta taron sun bayyana cewa karramawar ta zo a daidai lokaci, tare da fatan zai ci gaba da zama abin koyi ga matasa a fannin lafiya da jagoranci.
